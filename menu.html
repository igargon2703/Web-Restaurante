02Signature dishes

Five dishes
that tell our story

Created over almost three decades, they appear in one or both tasting menus.

  1. 01

    Ajoblanco with Motril red prawn

    A cold soup of Marcona almonds, garlic and Sacromonte olive oil, the recipe of Elena's grandmother. We serve it with a lightly grilled red prawn from Motril and green grapes marinated in fino sherry.

    Since
    1998
    Pairing
    Fino en rama, Sanlucar
    Allergens
    Nuts, crustaceans, sulphites
  2. 02

    Saffron sea bass baked in salt

    Wild sea bass baked whole in a crust of salt and orange peel, opened at the table and finished with a beurre blanc infused with saffron from La Mancha.

    Since
    2004
    Pairing
    Albariño, Rias Baixas
    Allergens
    Fish, diary
  3. 03

    Ajoblanco with Motril red prawn

    A tribute to the Moorish kitchens of the Alhambra: pigeon slowly cooked with ras el hanout, wrapped in crisp warka pastry with toasted almonds and a dusting of cinnamon.

    Since
    2009
    Pairing
    Garnacha, Sierra de Gredos
    Allergens
    Gluten, nuts
  4. 04

    Alpujarra lamb with Moorish spices

    Lamb from the Vilchez family farm, roasted over holm-oak embers and glazed with cumin, coriander and preserved lemon. Served with a cauliflower couscous and mint.

    Since
    2018
    Pairing
    Tempranillo, Granada highlands
    Allergens
    Celery, sulphites
  5. 05

    Azahar

    The dessert that gives the house its name: a light orange blossom cream, Sacromonte olive oil, candied bitter orange and a crisp tuitle of Alpujarra honey.

    Since
    1998
    Pairing
    Moscatel, Málaga
    Allergens
    Dairy, eggs, gluten

03 Good to know

Dietary needs

We adapt every menu with pleasure. Tell us in advance and the kitchen will prepare.

Do you offer vegetarian or vegan menus?

Yes. Both tasting menus have a vegetarian version, and a vegan version of Al-Ándalus is available. Please let us know at least 48 hours before your visit.

How do you handle allergies?

Every dish is listed with its allergens following EU Regulation 1169/2011. Tell us about any allergy in the reservation form and your waiter will confirm it at the table.

Can different guests choose different menus?

To keep the rhythm of the evening, the same tasting menu is served to the whole table. Pairings can be chosen individually.
